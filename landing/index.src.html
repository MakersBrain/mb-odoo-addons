The idea

One thread, end to end

Most workshop software gives you an inventory list, a till, and a spreadsheet for the rest. The pieces never connect, so the question you actually have — which bucket of glaze went on the mugs that sold at Vannes in March — has no answer. MakersBrain is built the other way round: one identity that survives every step.

A material arrives

A sack of stoneware, a bucket of glaze. It carries its supplier lot, and glazes carry whether they are food-safe — because that decides what you are allowed to make with them.

mb_catalogue_sync · mb_inventory_capture

A piece is made

Thrown, trimmed, assembled. The work centres are real resources you queue for, so a fourteen-hour firing does not get chopped across three working days by a calendar that thinks you go home.

mb_workshop_base · mb_ceramics_workflow

It is fired

Bisque, then glaze. The firing is a record with its programme and its load, and if your controller is a ROHDE, MakersBrain reads the schedule off it rather than asking you to retype it.

mb_ceramics_firing · mb_kiln_bridge

It gets a label

A code, a QR and a printed label that comes out of a thermal printer at the right physical size. The QR resolves to that exact piece — not the product, the piece.

mb_label

It sells

Scan the label at a market and the till sells that serial. Or it goes out on dépôt-vente and you can still say where it is and whether it has sold.

mb_label_pos · mb_depot · mb_pos_sumup

It is declared

The market day totals up against the stall fee, the fuel and the hours. The quarter totals up against the URSSAF rates in force on the declaration date — not today's.

mb_commercial_operations · l10n_fr_micro_urssaf
What it keeps track of

The parts you would otherwise keep in your head

Every one of these is a module you can install or leave out. A workshop that sells at markets but owns no kiln takes the label branch and none of the firing branch.

Pieces and labels

Serial identity for one-off work, QR codes that decode to the exact piece, and label layouts you edit in the browser and print on a thermal printer.

mb_label, mb_label_pos

Firing

Kilns as schedulable resources on a 24/7 calendar, firing records with programmes and loads, and a connector for ROHDE controllers.

mb_ceramics_firing, mb_kiln_bridge

Food-contact compliance

A mug carries the lead and cadmium limits of Directive 84/500/EEC. A decorative plate does not. The distinction is declared once and everything else follows from it.

mb_workshop_base

Consignment

Dépôt-vente as a first-class thing: what is out, at which shop, since when, and what has sold without anyone telling you.

mb_depot

Markets and profitability

What a market day actually earned once the stall, the travel and the hours are counted against it. Some of them do not earn anything, and it is better to know which.

mb_commercial_operations

Payments

SumUp as a payment provider at the till and against invoices, so the card machine you already own is the one the books know about.

mb_payment_sumup, mb_pos_sumup

French paperwork

Micro-entreprise VAT handling and URSSAF declarations, computed from the rates that applied in the period rather than the ones showing today.

l10n_fr_micro_enterprise, l10n_fr_micro_urssaf

Capture

Photograph a delivery or a supplier invoice and let it read the barcodes, the products and the lot numbers instead of typing them.

mb_inventory_capture, mb_invoice_capture
Built on Odoo

Not a box you can be locked out of

MakersBrain is a set of addons for Odoo 19 Community, licensed LGPL-3 throughout. That is a deliberate choice with consequences you can check rather than take on trust.

If we stopped tomorrow, your workshop would still open in the morning.

One database per workshop

Your records are not rows in a shared table keyed by a customer id. They are a database, and you can have a copy of it.

Real accounting underneath

Invoicing, stock moves, manufacturing and point of sale are Odoo's, not a reimplementation. The specialist parts sit on top of them.

Open source, and inspectable

Every module is LGPL-3. Nothing about how your firings, lots or declarations are computed is hidden from you.

Where it is today

Honestly: early, and specific

This is not a finished product with a marketing page pretending otherwise. It is being built in a working ceramics studio, and it is furthest along in the places that studio needed first.

Working Ceramics, deeply

Firing, glaze chemistry, food-contact rules and piece identity are built and tested. Other crafts can use the label, consignment, market and paperwork branches today.

Working France, first

Micro-entreprise and URSSAF are built because that is the regime in front of us. Other countries are not done and are not pretended to be.

Verified 634 server tests

Run on every push, alongside a clean install and an in-place upgrade against an empty database. The label QR is checked by decoding a real generated image.

Not yet No open sign-up

There is no self-serve checkout and no price list, because neither would be honest yet. Early access is a conversation.
