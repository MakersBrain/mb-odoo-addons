# France — Micro-Entreprise

This Odoo 19 addon prepares an idempotent French franchise-en-base tax setup
without replacing the economic VAT rates stored on products.

Only after an Accounting Administrator uses the setup wizard or preparation
action, it prepares for the selected French fiscal company:

- separate 0% goods and service sales taxes;
- EN16931 category `E` and exemption code `VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE`;
- the invoice note `TVA non applicable, article 293 B du CGI`;
- mappings from active or archived standard-category (`S`) French sales VAT;
- a France-only automatic fiscal position.

Accounting administrators switch the company under **Invoicing → Settings →
Taxes → Micro-enterprise VAT regime**. **Activate franchise en base** enables
the automatic domestic mapping. **Activate VAT liable** disables it and lets
the original product taxes apply again. The operation records its date and
user and never rewrites products or existing accounting documents.

Foreign transactions retain Odoo's normal fiscal-position handling. Users
should keep a valid country on every customer because Odoo does not
automatically determine any fiscal position for a partner without a country.
Products without a configured economic tax remain taxless: the addon relies on
Odoo's native fiscal-position computation and does not invent or remap taxes on
an invoice line after that computation.

Factur-X files are generated by Odoo's native `account_edi_ubl_cii` exporter
and may be downloaded or handed to any external approved-platform connector.
The addon does not require Odoo's PDP service. For a franchise seller without
a VAT number, the native preflight accepts the real company registration
identifier that Odoo already emits as EN16931 BT-30; it never fabricates a VAT
identifier.

Accounting managers can maintain the complete sole-proprietor profile from
**Invoicing → Configuration → French EI Setup**. The page writes through to
Odoo's standard company, contact, registration, and bank-account records,
stores the APE/activity metadata, applies the selected VAT regime and effective
date, and displays a Factur-X readiness checklist.
